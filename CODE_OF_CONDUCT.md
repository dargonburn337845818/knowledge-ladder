# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to keep this project a welcoming, respectful space for everyone,
regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and patient with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy: do not ask for or share personal credentials.
- Report bugs with enough detail to reproduce, but never include secrets.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate comments, close issues, or block
contributors who violate this policy. If you see a problem, contact the
maintainers privately through the repository's security reporting path.
